# Open Questions and Evidence Gaps — Scenario B

These are questions to clarify; no answers should be invented.

1. Which roles may view scheduled appointments?
2. What appointment details may each role see?
3. Which support services and appointment types are offered?
4. Can students request appointments directly, or does a staff member confirm requests?
5. What information is necessary to record for an appointment?
6. Who may create, change or cancel appointments?
7. What is the agreed cancellation/change policy?
8. Are reminders required? If so, who receives them, when, and through what channel?
9. What should happen if no suitable appointment is available?
10. Which accessibility criteria and usability success measures will be used?
