## Summary

- Requirements that depend on unconfirmed rules: FR08 (instant booking or staff approval, Q09), FR11 (status values, Q04 and Q09), FR12 (cancellation policy, Q01) and NFR05 (the 3-minute target is a proposed value).
- NFR01 was refined in Week 8 to name the fields collected, instead of "agreed" information.
- v0.5: FR08, FR09, FR11, FR14 and NFR05 reworded from "booked" and "confirmed" to "request" and "sent", to match the redrawn prototype and Q09.
- v0.6 (Lab 10 quality audit): FR01 reworded to name support staff and the selected date, so it matches UC-D01 and AD01. NFR03 reworded to list the WCAG 2.2 criteria it checks, so it can be verified.
- CR01 (reminders) is only partly covered by FR14, which sends a confirmation when a request is sent. Reminders before the appointment are not covered and stay a candidate.
