## 4. What is my own work?

The project is my own work, developed using the knowledge and skills gained through lectures, practical labs and independent research.

I was responsible for researching the scenario, understanding the requirements, analysing the information and making decisions about how the proposed system should work.

AI tools helped me explore ideas and gave examples of possible diagrams and approaches, but I did not copy and submit their outputs. I used the AI-assisted diagram drafts as references, then recreated the diagrams myself in draw.io and made my own adjustments and improvements to their structure, content and accuracy.

I also independently organised and maintained my GitHub repository, created and edited the project files, reviewed the requirements and made decisions about the final content.

I treated AI suggestions as guidance and checked them against my research, lecture materials, practical labs and project brief. Where information was uncertain, I tried to separate confirmed requirements from assumptions and open questions, so that nothing unconfirmed was presented as fact.

I take full responsibility for the final requirements, analysis, modelling decisions, documentation and submission. The final work reflects my own understanding, judgement and interpretation of the project requirements.
